## Feed Predictor — Grower bags (user-reported)
`app.js` `feedForecast()`:
- Batches are now split into living pools via the authoritative count engine:
  fattener / breeder / farm-use / unassigned (sold & dead heads excluded).
- **Departure-aware simulation** (the real fix):
  - fattener heads leave at **day 160** (market — same convention as the
    Production Forecast);
  - breeder heads leave at **day 90** (breeder release);
  - farm-use / unassigned keep the previous 180-day cap.
  - A head only produces bags on days it is still on the farm **and** inside
    that feed's age window (Grower = day 71–120).
- Old behaviour counted every head through age 180, so fatteners going to market
  at day 160 kept "eating" up to 20 extra days per batch — Grower/Finisher bags
  were inflated when the market date fell inside the horizon (and the "today"
  headcount included already-sold pigs in the Feeding Guide).
- Feeding Guide (`feeding-guide.js`) and the batch feed chips also use
  `liveHeadsFor()` now (sold heads no longer fed/planned).

## v109 — Feed Predictor / Feeding Guide consistency (2026-08-25, follow-up)
**Problem reported:** with a 30-day forecast, Feeding Guide said Grower ≈ 15.4 bags
(order 11) while the Feed Predictor said 120.2 bags (order 116).
**Root cause:** the predictor used a fixed age-based simulation (2.1 kg/head/day
for every day a pig is 71–120 days old) and ignored the manager-configured stage
plan + per-batch "consumed bags" tracking, so it replanned a full 30 days of
Grower for pigs that had already eaten most of their grower allocation.
**Fix:**
- `feedForecast()` now delegates to the SAME engine as the Feeding Guide
  (`computeFeedPlan(period)`) whenever the guide is configured — per-head
  planned bags per stage, "consumed bags" per batch, sow day-110 split, boar
  ration. The predictor and the guide now report the identical 30-day number.
- Age-derived stage progress: batches with no manual consumed entry auto-advance
  by their real age and configured stage durations (a 100-day-old pig is no
  longer assumed to be on Pre Starter).
- Unconfigured farms keep the age-based standard-rate engine (market day 160 /
  breeder day 90 / 180-day cap) as a fallback; both paths return bags + kg.
- Feed Predictor banner explains which model produced the number.
- SW cache bumped to v109.

## v110 — Fattener Center live-count readjustment (2026-08-25, follow-up)
**Problem reported:** Fattener Center cards showed "Assigned fattener" at the GROSS
allocation while ALIVE was lower — e.g. P4 Charlotte (5M/5F born, 10 assigned, 1
death) showed 10 assigned / 9 alive; P00-Aida (9M/5F born = 14, 5 deaths) showed
14 assigned / 9 alive.
**Root cause:** `assignedG()` summed `fattener` ledger rows minus reservations only;
it never deducted mortality or sales, and the fallback in `aliveHeads` hid the drift.
**Fix:**
- Fattener Center now computes "Assigned fattener" from the LIVING fattener pool
  (`counts()` engine): allocation − attributable deaths/sales − drained
  unattributed deaths, capped per gender by living headcount — identical to the
  Batch Hub "Fattener" stat. New `window.fattenerLivingFor(batch)` helper used by
  the card (♂/♀), the herd header, batch filtering (`herdHeads`) and market pickers.
- Production Forecast "Fattener Market Readiness" heads also use the living pool
  (dead heads no longer show as eating toward market).
- SW cache bumped to v110.
